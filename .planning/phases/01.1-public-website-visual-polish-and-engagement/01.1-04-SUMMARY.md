## Decisions Made

- Added a server-only legal navigation selector because no canonical eligible legal DTO existed; client components never reconstruct legal destinations.
- Kept the Header as the sole interactive shell island and used native links/button semantics without ARIA menu roles or a focus trap.
- Used repository-owned inline SVG glyphs with source provenance, avoiding packages, embeds, feeds, SDKs, and third-party runtime scripts.
