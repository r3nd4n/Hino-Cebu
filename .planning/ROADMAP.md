# Roadmap: Hino Cebu Digital Growth Website

## Overview

This roadmap turns the validated Next.js MVP into a production-ready commercial website without rebuilding its existing routes, components, content architecture, or integration seams. Work proceeds from approved business and operational contracts to reliable lead delivery, truthful publishing and privacy controls, behavioral release evidence, and a real-environment launch gate. One deliberately bounded post-launch phase then uses reconciled lead and search evidence to grow Cebu-specific authority. Provider selection, legal approval, verified business facts, production accounts, the final domain, and operational ownership remain explicit decisions or dependencies until approved.

## Phases

- [ ] **Phase 1: Production Contracts and Executable Configuration** - Establish approved production facts, owners, accounts, integration contracts, and fail-closed environment rules.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Lead Correctness, Durable Acceptance, and Recovery** - Make every existing inquiry journey accurate, abuse-resistant, durably accepted, observable, and recoverable.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Truthful Publishing, Privacy, Measurement, and Security** - Enforce approved content, consent-aware measurement, PII-safe telemetry, hardened delivery, and mobile performance.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Behavioral Verification and Release Gate** - Prove critical behavior in automation and protected preview before production promotion is possible.
- [ ] **Phase 5: Real-Environment Launch and Operations Handoff** - Validate the approved production estate end to end and complete the operational launch gate.
- [ ] **Phase 6: Evidence-Led Local Authority Growth** - Use post-launch evidence to publish one controlled slice of Cebu-specific proof, guidance, and conversion improvement.

### Phase 2: Lead Correctness, Durable Acceptance, and Recovery

**Goal**: Visitors can complete every existing inquiry journey with honest outcomes while operators can reliably deliver, reconcile, and recover each accepted lead.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: LEAD-01, LEAD-02, LEAD-03, LEAD-04, LEAD-05, LEAD-06, LEAD-07, LEAD-08, LEAD-09, LEAD-10
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A visitor can submit every full and compact sales, service, parts, fleet, financing, and campaign form, and receives accessible field-specific guidance for invalid or tampered input.
  2. Each accepted inquiry has trusted server-derived context, bounded attribution, and a stable identifier, and the visitor sees success only after the approved system confirms durable, recoverable acceptance.
  3. A timeout or delivery outage produces an honest retryable failure with verified contact alternatives, never a false confirmation or duplicate downstream delivery.
  4. Public forms withstand the approved abuse controls while legitimate users can still submit without relying on client-side validation as the security boundary.
  5. Authorized operators can observe PII-safe lifecycle states, reconcile departmental delivery, replay recoverable failures safely, and investigate terminal/dead-letter outcomes by stable identifier.

### Phase 3: Truthful Publishing, Privacy, Measurement, and Security

**Goal**: Public content and marketing technology operate only with approved evidence, consent, origins, assets, privacy safeguards, and performance limits.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: GOV-01, GOV-02, GOV-03, GOV-04, GOV-05, GOV-06, GOV-07, GOV-08, GOV-09, GOV-10
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. An editor can publish a business, product, promotion, delivery, guide, or campaign record only with required source, ownership, approval, Cebu applicability, review/expiry, rights, and release evidence; unsafe publishable records fail the build.
  2. Withheld or ineligible content is absent consistently from pages, navigation, links, metadata, structured data, and the sitemap.
  3. A visitor can read the approved privacy notice and make, revisit, or withdraw granular optional analytics and advertising consent without blocking an essential inquiry.
  4. Optional tags stay absent without valid identifiers and required consent, use one approved control path when enabled, and emit allow-listed journey context without lead PII.
  5. Production pages enforce approved security headers and mobile performance budgets while serving only authorized, optimized, responsive fonts and images with appropriate alternative text and dimensions.

### Phase 4: Behavioral Verification and Release Gate

**Goal**: A release candidate cannot advance unless automated and human evidence proves critical commercial, accessibility, SEO, security, configuration, and recovery behavior.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: QUAL-01, QUAL-02, QUAL-03, QUAL-04, QUAL-05, QUAL-06, QUAL-07, QUAL-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. Deterministic unit and integration tests exercise environment, publication, origin, attribution, finder, and every lead type/variant across acceptance, rejection, timeout, transport, retry, idempotency, and replay behavior.
  2. Desktop and mobile browser journeys prove navigation, inquiry flows, finder behavior, attribution, accessible status/errors, verified contact actions, and failure recovery.
  3. Automated and documented human checks provide WCAG 2.2 AA evidence for keyboard, focus, screen-reader, contrast, reduced-motion, touch-target, and responsive behavior.
  4. SEO checks prove canonical, index/noindex, robots, sitemap, redirect, social, breadcrumb, and structured-data behavior for each environment.
  5. CI and protected-preview promotion stop when code quality, build, dependency/security, secret/configuration, accessibility, performance, critical-journey, approval, or operational-runbook evidence is missing or failing.

### Phase 5: Real-Environment Launch and Operations Handoff

**Goal**: The approved production domain, accounts, integrations, monitoring, and owners prove the complete commercial journey before acquisition is scaled.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 4 and approval/access for the final domain, commercial Vercel project, lead provider, analytics/tag accounts, Search Console, Google Business Profile, and named operators
**Requirements**: LAUN-01, LAUN-02, LAUN-03, LAUN-04, LAUN-05, LAUN-06, LAUN-07, LAUN-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. The approved commercial Vercel project isolates preview and production, protects secrets and permissions, uses the intended runtime, and exposes spend controls to its named owner.
  2. The final HTTPS domain produces correct canonical, social, sitemap, structured-data, redirect, and robots behavior with no localhost or preview leakage, and Search Console verifies ownership, sitemap, crawl state, and redirect coverage.
  3. Website branch identity, hours, phone, directions, domain, and landing target match the verified Google Business Profile.
  4. A controlled non-PII production inquiry proves durable acceptance, departmental delivery, reconciliation, alerting, and cleanup; consent/tag diagnostics also prove approved activation, attribution, and PII-free events.
  5. Named operators successfully receive actionable uptime, error, lead-delivery, dead-letter, and configuration alerts and complete the recorded smoke test, escalation, replay, rollback, configuration audit, stakeholder acceptance, and handoff before spend scales.

### Phase 6: Evidence-Led Local Authority Growth

**Goal**: After the production launch gate, the business can run one governed, measurable growth loop using verified Cebu evidence and reconciled lead outcomes.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 5; production lead reconciliation, factual/content approvals, customer permissions, response capacity, and sufficient Search Console and journey evidence must be available
**Requirements**: GROW-01, GROW-02, GROW-03, GROW-04, GROW-05, GROW-06, GROW-07
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):

  1. A content owner can publish permissioned Cebu delivery stories and reviewed Truck Guide or support resources whose facts, application context, ownership, review dates, related products, and inquiry paths are verified.
  2. A visitor using the truck finder receives one to three locally reviewed model-family recommendations whose reasons reflect every relevant answer and clearly require final consultation.
  3. Marketing and operations can reconcile source, campaign, landing page, interest, durable lead identifier, and a minimal governed qualified-lead status without sending PII to analytics.
  4. A named owner follows a recurring workflow that keeps Google Business Profile and website hours, contacts, photos, landing targets, and branch updates consistent.
  5. The next content or campaign slice is selected from qualified-lead outcomes, Search Console demand, journey and call/directions intent, response capacity, and approval readiness before spend, content volume, or tooling expands.
