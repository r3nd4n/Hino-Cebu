# Project Research Summary

**Project:** Hino Cebu Website  
**Domain:** Commercial vehicle dealership website  
**Researched:** 2026-08-26  
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Executive Summary

Build a content-led Next.js site that makes local Cebu truck discovery and contact immediate. Keep commercial facts typed and configurable, use truck-dominant visual composition, and treat the lead pipeline as a server-side operational boundary rather than a front-end form.

The major delivery risk is not framework selection: it is publishing unverified facts or presenting a visually generic dealership. The roadmap therefore establishes data/design contracts first, builds the homepage as the conversion slice, then adds discovery, lead routing, and launch hardening.

## Key Findings

- **Stack:** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Vercel, Zod, Google Sheets API, Resend, and server-validated Turnstile.
- **Table stakes:** responsive local contact, truck discovery, quote capture, semantic SEO/accessibility, and safe handling of inquiry failures.
- **Differentiator:** a premium truck-led hero with an integrated quote action, business-use guidance, and credible Cebu aftersales context.
- **Watch out for:** unverified branch facts, provider-only happy paths, client-only anti-bot controls, spreadsheet formula injection, and visual-template drift.

## Roadmap Implications

1. Start with contracts, design system, and verified/configurable data boundaries.
2. Deliver the homepage early as an end-to-end conversion slice.
3. Extend reusable patterns into truck, support, and local-information routes.
4. Complete the secure lead pipeline before declaring conversion functionality done.
5. Finish with measurable quality, SEO, documentation, and commercial launch checks.

## Gaps to Resolve During Implementation

- Dealer legal entity, authorized brand/image assets, email recipients, map listing, and locally supported vehicle ranges.
- Final privacy/terms language and analytics-consent obligations.
