## Delivery guidance

- Supply original files with usage rights and descriptive filenames.
- Include model, location, subject, photographer/source, permission status, and intended alt-text context.
- Avoid text baked into photography; campaign text should remain HTML.
- Keep focal subjects clear at mobile and desktop crops.
- Convert approved raster assets to optimized AVIF/WebP during publishing while retaining originals outside the deployed bundle.
